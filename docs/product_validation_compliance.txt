# Product Validation Compliance Rules

This document contains the compliance rules and validation requirements for product data validation.

---

## 1. Image Validation Requirements

All product images must meet the following minimum specifications:

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|
| Minimum Resolution | 1920×1080 (Full HD) |

### 1.1 General Image Rules

- **Minimum resolution**: 1920×1080. Images below this threshold are **invalid**.
- Images meeting or exceeding 1920×1080 pixels are considered **valid**.
- Images should not be blurred — blurred images are **invalid**.
- The dominant object in the image must be the product mentioned in the title. If not, the image is **invalid**.
- Product title and description must match the contents of the image. A mismatch is a **failure**.
- Products without an image URL are marked as validation **failed**.
- Images that fail to download or process are marked as validation **failed**.

### 1.2 Main Image Requirements

- Must clearly show the item being sold.
- **No text overlays or watermarks** are permitted on the main image.
- The image must correctly represent the product described in the title.
- **Size chart**: Required for apparel products — must be included in supplemental images.

### 1.3 Background & Aspect Ratio by Category

| Category | Background | Aspect Ratio |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| Ready to Wear | White background required | Some deviation from 1:1 is permitted |
| Lifestyle Products | Non-white background permitted | Must be exactly 1:1 |

### 1.4 Alternate Image Requirements

Alternate (supplemental) images are subject to the same general quality rules as the main image and additionally must satisfy:

- All alternate images must meet the **minimum resolution of 1920×1080**.
- Alternate images must not be blurred or fail to load — any such image is **invalid**.
- Alternate images **may contain lifestyle or contextual backgrounds** (not restricted to white).
- Text overlays and watermarks are **not permitted** on any alternate image.
- Each alternate image must be **distinct** — duplicate images across main and alternate slots are **not allowed**.
- For **apparel products**, at least one alternate image must display the **size chart**.
- Alternate images should provide **additional product perspectives** (e.g. back, side, detail, in-use shots) — images that add no new information are considered **low quality**.
- The product shown in alternate images must be **consistent** with the main image and the product title.

## 3. Attribute Validation Rules

### 3.1 Validation Logic

1. **Check Product Type Existence**: If product type is not found, validation fails
2. **Determine Required Attributes**: Combine common attributes with product-type-specific attributes
3. **Check for Missing Attributes**: Attributes that are not present in the product data
4. **Check for Empty Attributes**: Attributes that exist but have `null` or empty string `""` values

### 3.2 Validation Status

| Status | Condition |
|--------|-----------|
| **Success** | All required attributes are present and have non-empty values |
| **Failed** | One or more required attributes are missing or empty |

### 3.3 Validation Response Fields

- `status`: "success" or "failed"
- `valid`: boolean (true/false)
- `message`: Human-readable validation message
- `missing_attributes`: List of attributes not present in product data
- `empty_attributes`: List of attributes with null or empty values
- `required_attributes`: Complete list of required attributes for the product type
- `provided_attributes`: List of attributes actually provided in product data

## 4. Compliance Notes

### 4.1 Choking Hazard Compliance

Products require a `choking_hazard` attribute for safety compliance. This is required across all defined product types.

### 4.2 California Prop 65 Compliance

Product type `3_14_63` requires the `prop_65` attribute for California Proposition 65 compliance related to warnings about chemicals known to cause cancer or reproductive harm.

### 4.3 PFAS Compliance

Product type `3_14_63` requires the `containsPFAS` attribute to indicate whether the product contains per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS).

### 4.4 Perishable Products

All product types require the `perishable_indicator` attribute to indicate whether the product is perishable.

### 4.5 Large/Heavy Items (LTL)

Product type `3_14_63` requires the `is_ltl_item` attribute to indicate if the product requires less-than-truckload (LTL) shipping.

## 7. Extended Validation Rules

### 7.1 Category Correctness

- The P1:P2:P3 category hierarchy must be logically consistent with the product image, title, description, and features.
- A mismatch between the assigned category and the product content is a validation **failure**.

---

### 7.2 Variant Rules

- Each variant must have a **unique color and size combination**.
- Variants must be **grouped by color**; sizes must be unique within a color group.
- Size value **`000` is not permitted**.
- Duplicate variants (identical color + size) are **not allowed**.
- The variant image must logically match the color supplied by the vendor.

---

### 7.3 Restricted / Prohibited Categories (Vendor Agreements)

Products in the following categories must be **rejected upfront** due to exclusive vendor agreements:

| Category | Competing Vendor | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| Baby Gear | Key baby gear vendor | Definition of "Baby Gear" requires further clarification |
| Team / Licensed Sports Products | Fanatics | All team-branded merchandise is excluded |
| Beauty Products | Sephora | All beauty-related products are excluded |

---

### 7.4 Brand Consistency

- The brand displayed in product images must match the brand selected in the marketplace (Mirakl).
- A brand mismatch across any of the following signals is a validation **failure**:
  - Main image vs. brand field
  - Title vs. brand field
  - Description or features vs. brand field

---

### 7.5 Seller Approval Scope

- A seller should only be permitted to list products within the categories they are approved for.
- Listings outside the seller's approved category scope must be **rejected**.
